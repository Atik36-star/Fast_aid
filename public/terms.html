Terms & Volunteer Service Guidelines

Last updated: November 2026 • Applies to all FastAid responders and users

◆

1. Volunteer Eligibility & Verification

Volunteers must complete identity and basic first-aid or medical credential verification before accepting live emergency requests. Unverified accounts can browse guidelines and configure availability settings only.

◆

2. Response Commitment & Focus Mode

Once a volunteer accepts an incoming request, they agree to proceed promptly to the patient's shared location and keep status updates accurate across Accepted, On the Way, Arrived, and Completed stages.

◆

3. Live Location & Privacy

During an active response, real-time location data is shared strictly between the assigned volunteer and the requesting patient through the FastAid tracking interface until the case is marked completed.

◆

4. Scope of Emergency Assistance

FastAid connects nearby trained responders for immediate first-aid stabilization. Volunteers must prioritize personal safety and coordinate with local ambulance or hospital emergency services whenever advanced clinical care is required.
